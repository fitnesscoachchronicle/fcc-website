Why New PTs Keep Asking Me About This

Somewhere along the way, "REPs points" became shorthand for a good qualification. I still hear it from people considering a career change today.

The truth is simpler than most explainers make it sound. REPs hasn't run as an independent system for years. Understanding what replaced it matters more than memorising the old name.

What REPs Actually Was, and What Changed

REPs, the Register of Exercise Professionals, used to be the industry's main registration body. Trainers listed themselves there to prove they were qualified and insured.

In 2020, REPs merged into CIMSPA's Exercise and Fitness Directory. CIMSPA is now the sector's professional register, and its only chartered body.

That charter, granted by the Privy Council, gives CIMSPA a level of formal recognition REPs never had. When people ask me "CIMSPA or REPs," the honest answer is that REPs isn't really a separate choice anymore.

What CIMSPA Endorsement Actually Confirms

CIMSPA endorsement isn't a marketing badge. It confirms a course has been checked against professional standards employers and insurers actually rely on.

That's separate from Ofqual regulation, which confirms the qualification itself is formally recognised nationally. The strongest courses carry both.

How to Make Sure Your Course Actually Counts

Check the provider's CIMSPA endorsement directly on the CIMSPA website before you enrol anywhere. Don't just trust a logo on their homepage.

Confirm the qualification carries genuine Ofqual regulation, not just a generic "accredited" claim. Then ask how many hours of real practical assessment the course includes.

If a provider can't answer those three questions clearly, that tells you something important before you've spent a penny.

Common Questions About CIMSPA and REPs

  • What is the difference between CIMSPA and REPs?

    REPs, the old Register of Exercise Professionals, was absorbed into CIMSPA's Exercise and Fitness Directory in 2020. CIMSPA is now the sector's only chartered professional body, holding a Royal Charter from the Privy Council. REPs no longer runs as a separate registration route.

  • Do I need to be CIMSPA registered to work as a PT in Scotland?

    You don't legally need to be, but in practice most gyms and insurers expect it. A CIMSPA-endorsed Level 3 qualification is what I check for first on any CV that comes across my desk at Platform.

  • Is a REPs-registered PT qualification still valid today?

    The underlying qualification is still valid if it was Ofqual regulated at the time. What changed is the registration route. CIMSPA's Exercise and Fitness Directory replaced REPs, so trainers now register there instead.

  • What does CIMSPA endorsement actually check for?

    CIMSPA endorsement confirms a course meets the professional standards employers and insurers expect, covering both the training content and the assessment process. It's separate from Ofqual regulation, which confirms the qualification itself carries formal, nationally recognised status.

  • What do you actually check for when hiring a PT at Platform?

    CIMSPA endorsement and Ofqual regulation first, since they confirm the qualification is real. Then I watch how someone handles a live client, not their exam paper. A certificate gets you an interview. Coaching ability gets you the job.

  • Should I choose a course based on CIMSPA endorsement alone?

    It's the first filter, not the only one. Also check whether the course includes real practical assessment days and whether it teaches you to actually find and keep clients once you qualify. Our guide on personal trainer course cost in Edinburgh covers what else to weigh up before enrolling.
