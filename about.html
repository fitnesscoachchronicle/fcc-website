Our Story

Built by Gym Owners, for the Next Generation of PTs

The Fitness Coach Chronicle was founded by Jamie Henderson — PT for 11 years, owner of Platform Health & Fitness in Edinburgh, and the coach behind over 1,000 personal training clients. The FCC exists because of one conviction: becoming a great PT requires more than memorising anatomy textbooks. It requires learning from someone who has actually built this career from the ground up.

Jamie has lived every version of this industry. Employed in a gym. Self-employed building a client base. Working online. A year-long internship in elite sport. Then founding Platform and growing it to £250k in annual revenue within three years. He's hired PTs, mentored them, and watched first-hand what separates the ones who build lasting careers from the ones who stall out six months after qualifying. The FCC was built from that experience.

Too many PTs leave their qualification with a certificate and nothing else. No client acquisition plan. No pricing strategy. No understanding of how to actually run the business. Every FCC course is built to close that gap: get you qualified, confident, and earning — not just ticking a regulatory box.

Our Credentials & Accreditations

When you graduate from The FCC, you hold qualifications that gym employers across the UK recognise and respect.

CIMSPA Endorsed

The Chartered Institute for the Management of Sport and Physical Activity is the UK's professional body for fitness. CIMSPA endorsement is the gold standard. It's what employers check first.

OFQUAL Regulated

Our Level 3 qualification is regulated by Ofqual, the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation. This means your qualification is nationally recognised and carries real weight with employers.

Active Industry Professionals

Our tutors aren't academics. They're working coaches and gym owners. The FCC meets CIMSPA's standards for tutor industry experience, ensuring you learn from people who are still actively coaching.
